# Belsoft Solutions

**Your AI & automation transformation partner.** We audit how your company runs, build the AI and automation that fixes it, and train your team to own it, then stay embedded as you grow.

Based in Lisbon, Portugal, working with teams across Europe and the Middle East.

## 🧭 How We Partner

1. **Audit**: We sit with your team, map every workflow and tool, and measure where time, money, and clients are being lost.
2. **Roadmap**: A written transformation plan: what to automate, which AI agents to deploy, what it's worth, and in what order.
3. **Build**: We build and deploy the agents and automation ourselves, inside the tools your team already uses, with a human in control.
4. **Train**: Every team learns to run, trust, and extend what we built. Adoption is the product.
5. **Partner**: We stay embedded, tracking results, shipping the next automation, and evolving your systems as you grow.
